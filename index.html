A small, open-source DMG emulator

Browser Boy

Load a Game Boy ROM you own and play it in your browser. ROM files stay on your device.

Choose a .gb or .gbc ROM to begin.

Controls

← ↑ ↓ → move · Z A · X B · Enter Start · Shift Select

This lightweight emulator implements the Game Boy CPU, cartridge banking, timers, joypad input, and DMG background/sprite rendering. Compatibility varies; audio and some advanced hardware are not implemented.
